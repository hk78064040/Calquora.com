    Frequently asked questions

    How do I calculate a percentage?

    Type the number and press %, which divides it by 100. To find 15% of 80, enter 80 × 15 % and press =, which gives 12.

    What is the difference between Deg and Rad?

    It sets the angle unit for sin, cos and tan. Deg uses degrees (a full circle is 360) and Rad uses radians (a full circle is 2π). The default is degrees.

    What is the difference between ln and log?

    ln is the natural logarithm (base e) and log is the common logarithm (base 10). For example, log(1000) is 3.

    Can I use my keyboard?

    Yes. Type digits and the symbols + − * / ^ ( ) . %, press Enter to calculate, Backspace to delete and Escape to clear.
